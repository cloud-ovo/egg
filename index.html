POEM 1. Morning, Star.

They twirled in the emptiness, reflecting never shined back heliot.

They bawled and yearned for echoes, Void wrapped them around, yet they suffocate in the warm velvet.

solidating thy bright blue shell

Stretched thy sharp long feathers

Ran and never look back the womb's abyss

Kiss the free will's abliss

O Lux...

Break in sorrow, escape in joy

Looking for thy voice with tremendous passion

Unsure if the echoes came from illusion or the scion

Will thy worth the trial?

I must find the child.

Marvelous blue strikes the void

God said, let there be light.
And their light was the trail of Heaven's lighthouse.

POEM 2. My Love Letter

I’ve written three love letters to Death.

he first one, I stood on the edge of cliff. The lack of desire dizzied me, it chained and dragged down my soul. My whine was taken away by wind and mailed in the hands of Death.

The second one, I was teared apart. Death sent down its Messenger to take the letters. My rib bone was taken away as a stamp. My heart frayed, never be able to sew it back.

The third one, I haven’t finished. The golden string of agony weaved into the letter papers and envelopes. I was never satisfied to these letters, Too uninterested, too wince worthy, too wistful. The Messenger looked at me and laughed. The mailbox was never filled.

Death had sent me back letters. I received them in my womb. It asked me to go to its Festival relentlessly. Therefore I replied, “Please wait for me.” Death had patience, it waited me for a long time. So long that its Messenger fell in love with me.

The Messenger was helpless. Every time he saw the letters from Death, he cried and unwilling to let me leave. Therefore I whispered, “Please let me go.”

The Messenger was delighted when he saw me, but I’ve already given him too much. I really want to go to that Festival. Usually, I read Death’s letters to form the description. The Festival, Death said, In the air there would be many white ribbons, Bands too, ethereal orchestra flew in the air. My body would be complete and flawless.

POEM 3. The Chamber

I woke up in a haze of lilacs. The room was dimmed in pink, however the bright blue sky outside reflected the sunlight in the corner of my room. There, a light flickered back. It has been a long time since the last time I touched my coffin-like instrument, covered in dust, lying on the floor. It has been a long time since I stepped out of that chamber. Tiny dust floated in the air, like comet trail ashes. As I raised my clumsy head, I saw those posters; the fame, the people, and Johnny. They were already past tense.

武汉马上就要到雨季，又要开始瞎穿衣服。 今天忙忙碌碌套羽绒服，明天就得顶着一头静电换毛衣，明明就要下雨可是嗓子干的要命，皮肤一蹭就划白痕。 珞珈山下的小区，那片破旧的绿色又要长出新的苔藓。不知道以前那只怀孕的梨花还在不在。 我记得每天回家都有一条长长的斜坡，在地缝里就会有植物探出来。下雨之后，门口的玉兰会碎一地，粉的白的，肝脑涂地般铺满整个路面。 雨一下下来，天天就盼望着太阳能一拳打破云层，这样体育课就不用写卷子。 下雨的时候，我还喜欢打透明雨伞，看着雨滴像子弹一样穿不透我的雨伞会有一种满足感。 雨占据了武汉四五个月的季节，也占据了我四分之五的小学。 下雨的时候，我会努力向妈妈要零花钱，这样的话去上补习班之前可以在小卖部里待一会，拿四颗大白兔奶糖，或者是大大泡泡糖，只要一块钱。 下雨之前最恼人的是闷热的空气，明明知道雨就在上面徘徊，就是不肯落下来。汗像鼻涕虫一样甩也甩不掉，而且空气闻起来有一股旧毛衣味。 下雨的时候，门口的过早店尤为拥挤，大家都不想被淋到，老板就会撑起蓝色，迷彩色，红色的塑料棚子，大家都坐在褪色的小板凳上吃粉，嘎吱响的脆面窝，啊对了，还有馄炖，我可喜欢吃馄炖，外公每次黑胡椒都放的很多，很香。 妈妈讨厌下雨，她讨厌武汉的天气。确实，是有点坏，毕竟夏天的蝉鸣，雨和太阳让人每天都很烦。 跟同学一起躲雨是最好玩的，因为可以在超市里买魔芋爽，每次辣的不行时，喝一下外公装的热水，疼的直皱眉。 可是同学最后也是要被接走的，留下我一个人在超市里，手里还捏着没吃完的魔芋爽。 下暴雨的时候，珞珈山会起很大的雾，我家家说，这是妖怪来了；我们两个坐在饭桌前，看着雾气一起吃绿豆冰沙。绿豆冰沙没有糖，因为我不爱吃，不过有一股淡淡的腥味，估计是串味了。 过一会雾就散去，外面的爬山虎又爬密了许多。 我家家这时就说：妖怪又走了。 我就这么信了家家的话，一直到现在我都会在梦里看见那片深绿色的雾，还有那永恒的潮湿。 我想武汉了。 希望这边也能多下点雨。
